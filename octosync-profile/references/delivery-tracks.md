# Delivery tracks (full detail)

Two tracks. Both include bank-grade hosting, guaranteed uptime, and
complete orchestration of the automated system.

## How compute is priced — read before writing any copy

**Track 1 is all-in. Track 2 is metered. Metered pricing lives on
Track 2 and only on Track 2.**

Never itemize tokens, compute, or model costs in customer-facing copy —
not in outreach, not in a proposal, not in a follow-up. The sanctioned
line is:

> "You'll never see a token bill from us."

Fair use is stated in plain sight, never buried. When copy needs to
describe it, match the proposal's framing: the fair-use ceiling is
**benchmarked at onboarding**, sits at roughly **2× that benchmark**,
and any adjustment is **prospective only — never retroactive**. A
prospect asking "what if we go over?" gets that answer, not a hedge.

## Track 1 — Managed Workflows

**$600/month, all-in, per managed workflow.** AI compute included under
fair use. For standardized, tightly scoped workflows.

Named SKUs:

- **Managed Accounts Payable**
- **Managed Content Channel** — buyer-facing name: **content engine**
- **Managed Pipeline Research**

Each managed workflow is its own $600. **A second workflow is simply
another $600 — there is no bundle discount, ever.** Do not offer,
imply, or "check on" one.

Included per workflow:

- Up to 3 specialized AI agents (routine research, daily reporting,
  intelligent task management)
- One automated evaluation loop (e.g., email categorization, document
  QA)
- One standard integration into the customer's existing software
- 1 hour of dedicated technical support and workflow adjustments per
  month
- Engagement starts with a 30-minute discovery call to map the
  workflow and scope first automations

### Content engine scaling (inside Track 1, not a third track)

The content engine is a **productized Track 1 SKU**. Its scaling lives
inside the $600:

- **First channel included.** **+$250/month per additional channel.**
  A *channel* is a network destination.
- **Voice lives on a persona**, not on a channel. The first persona is
  included in onboarding; each additional voice/identity is a
  **one-time $250 voice setup** (published in the proposal as of
  2026-08-05).

**Channels and workflows are orthogonal.** Channels scale *within* a
content engine; a second workflow is a second $600. Do not blend the
two dimensions when quoting.

**Live channels are LinkedIn and X — exactly those two.** Do not name
Instagram (or any other network) in outreach copy until the image slice
ships; Instagram requires media that does not exist yet.

**One account is grandfathered** at $600 for two channels on
founding-partner terms. That is a closed, individually negotiated case:
**never generalize it to another prospect, never cite it as precedent,
and never name the account in outreach.** If a prospect references it,
route to a human.

## Track 2 — Custom Data & AI Architecture

**Starting at $1,500/month + metered compute.** For operations that
fall outside Track 1 — complex, proprietary, or regulated data; deeper
discovery; external service integration; multiple approval layers.

- **Gated by a fixed-price $1,500, 4-hour workflow audit.** Map
  technical requirements, regulatory constraints, internal business
  logic; define success criteria before any architecture is scoped.
  **A prospect who balks at the audit is politely disqualified** — the
  audit is the gate, not a negotiable step.
- Phase 2: build in short milestone-validated iterations. Scope changes
  are surfaced and re-priced before new work begins — pricing
  transparent start to finish.
- Engineers scoping the system are the engineers building it.

## How to use this reference

Routing rules when recommending an opportunity:

| Situation | Route |
|---|---|
| Standard SMB, one clear workflow | **Track 1 at $600** |
| Proprietary/regulated data, custom, or unclear scope | **Track 2**, via the $1,500 audit |
| Price resistance at $600 | **Review-only variant or a single-quarter pilot — never a price cut** |
| Resistance to the audit fee | **Disqualify politely** |

The ICP's stated budget band ($1,000–$1,500/month, see the profile's ICP
table) is unchanged and intentional: the land-one-expand-to-two path
lands at **$1,200 for two workflows**, which tops out *inside* the band
without discounting. That is the expansion story — sell a second
workflow, not a cheaper first one.
